# Bookified brand artwork

Generated with the built-in image_gen tool, using a genuine transparent background. Original artwork: `bookified-logo-source.png`. The app uses `public/brand/bookified-mark.png`; `app/favicon.ico` and `app/apple-icon.png` are size/format derivatives of the same mark. Rebuild derivatives with `node scripts/brand-assets.mjs`.

The wordmark uses locally bundled IBM Plex Serif. The three introductory card icons are custom two-tone SVG artwork in `components/premium-icons.tsx`, matching the espresso/brass palette. No paid icon subscription is required.

## Final generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Create one premium brand symbol for Bookified, a refined literary app for voice conversations with books. Symbol only, no words, no letters, no presentation sheet. A distinctive open-book silhouette with sculpted curved pages that subtly form a sound wave and the shape of a butterfly-like conversation opening. Elegant restrained editorial luxury; flat vector-like geometry, confident thick strokes, balanced negative space, beautifully precise optical symmetry, immediately legible at favicon size. Warm dark espresso brown #765744 with a small muted brass #B39973 accent on one inner page, matching an ivory literary website. Center a single isolated compact mark occupying 80 percent of a square canvas. Genuine transparent background. No shadows, no gradients, no 3D mockup, no fine ornament, no border box. Deliver a crisp original sophisticated publishing-house-quality logo symbol.
